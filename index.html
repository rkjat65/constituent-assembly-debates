The Objectives Resolution

13 December 194611:00 AM – 1:45 PMConstituent Assembly Hall, New Delhi

Jawaharlal Nehru

Member, Constituent Assembly

United Provinces

“
“This Constituent Assembly declares its firm and solemn resolve to proclaim India as an Independent Sovereign Republic...”
♙
— Jawaharlal Nehru13 December 1946
⌂

Summary of Key Points

  • ✺Nehru introduces the Objectives Resolution as the philosophical foundation of constitution-making.
  • ♧The Resolution places sovereignty in the people of India.
  • ◉It sets out justice, equality and safeguards for minorities and backward areas.
  • ⌁It links independent India with international peace and cooperation.
♘

✎ Editor’s Note

The Objectives Resolution provided the guiding political and moral framework for the Constitution that followed.

⚄ Context

The Resolution was moved in the opening phase of the Assembly, before independence and before the Constitution took final form.
