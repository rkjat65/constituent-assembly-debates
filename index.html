The First Sitting

9 December 194611:00 AMConstitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

Temporary Chairman

Constituent Assembly of India

“
I am deeply beholden to you for your having agreed to accept me as the first President of your Constituent Assembly...
♙
— Dr. Sachchidananda SinhaInaugural Address · 9 December 1946
⌂

Why this sitting matters

  • ✺The Constituent Assembly met for the first time at 11 AM in Constitution Hall, New Delhi.
  • ♧Acharya J. B. Kripalani invited Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha to take the Chair as temporary Chairman.
  • ◉Sinha situated Indian constitution-making in comparative and historical perspective.
  • ⌁The sitting closed with credentials and the signing of the Assembly Register.
♘

Opening

“ Kripalani invites Dr. Sinha to take the Chair as temporary Chairman.

Goodwill

“ Messages from the United States, China and Australia are read to the Assembly.

Petition

“ An election petition concerning the British Baluchistan seat is taken up.

Address

“ Sinha’s inaugural address surveys constitution-making abroad and the Indian demand for a Constituent Assembly.

Close

“ Deputy-chair nomination, condolence, credentials and signing of the Register complete the day’s agenda.
