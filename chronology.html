VOLUME III · RIGHTS UNDER PRESSURE

May 1947 — every liberty now has to survive its hard cases

The archive reaches 1 May. The rights discussion has moved beyond general declarations: inter-State commerce, forced labour and compulsory service, child labour, religious freedom, conversion, minority culture and educational institutions all force the Assembly to define the protected core of a right and the legitimate reach of State regulation.
