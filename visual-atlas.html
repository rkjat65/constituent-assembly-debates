Visual language

Faces, rooms and constitutional ideas become navigational memory.

The platform uses a consistent archival illustration system: parchment surfaces, engraved linework, restrained saffron and green, Ashoka-blue geometry, strong silhouettes and historically grounded labels. Source photographs may inform likeness, but the archive publishes transformed editorial illustrations rather than a random mix of stock headshots.
