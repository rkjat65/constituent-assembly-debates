How to read a genealogy

01Political promise

A constitutional objective appears before final legal wording exists.

02Institutional machinery

A committee or procedure is created to convert the promise into workable choices.

03Hard-case debate

Members test the proposal against social conflict, unintended consequences and State power.

04Final family

The adopted constitutional provision is treated as the destination, not retroactively projected onto every earlier speech.

Current limits are deliberate

The archive has not yet reached the full Draft Constitution and second/third reading stages. So this page currently maps only constitutional families whose underlying problems are already visible in processed sittings. As later debates are structured, each genealogy can acquire Draft Article numbers, amendments, voting outcomes and the final adopted text.
