President · Constituent Assembly

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

The archive introduces Rajendra Prasad through what he actually does in the record: he is elected permanent presiding officer, controls admissibility and sequencing, manages amendment procedure, schedules business and becomes the institutional continuity of the Assembly across sittings.

Indexed trajectory

11 DEC 1946

Elected permanent Chairman

Sachchidananda Sinha declares Rajendra Prasad duly elected after the nomination process is completed.

12–23 DEC 1946

The Chair becomes constitutional machinery

He repeatedly manages admissibility, amendment order, speaking time, scheduling, committee elections, Rules proceedings and adjournment.

20 JAN 1947

Defends the Assembly's representative character

Volume II opens with Rajendra Prasad placing community representation figures on record and answering criticism of the Assembly's composition.

21 JAN 1947

Controls the end of postponement

He manages Steering Committee election procedure and rules on the attempt to add new observations while Jayakar withdraws his amendment.

How to read this personality

Presiding officers often vanish in topic summaries because they do not always deliver the longest ideological speeches. A chronological archive makes their rulings visible because the shape of debate—who may move, amend, speak, withdraw or vote—is itself part of constitutional history.

Portrait and editorial summaries are presentation layers. Historical claims remain tied to the dated debate records linked below.
