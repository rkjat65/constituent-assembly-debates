Permanent Chairman · Constituent Assembly

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

The archive first introduces Rajendra Prasad at the institutional moment when the Assembly elects its permanent presiding officer. Rather than treating him as a static biography card, this profile grows with the sittings he chairs, the rulings he gives and the procedural transitions he oversees.

Indexed trajectory

11 DEC 1946

Elected permanent Chairman

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha declares Rajendra Prasad duly elected after the valid nomination process is read to the House.

11 DEC 1946

Conducted to the Chair

J. B. Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad conduct the elected President to the platform before the transition from the temporary Chair.

12 DEC 1946

Deliberation before debate

The sitting on the Objectives Resolution is postponed so members have more time to prepare.

13–23 DEC 1946

Presiding over the first constitutional session

Across the Objectives Resolution and Rules proceedings, the Chair repeatedly manages admissibility, amendment order, speaking time, scheduling, committee elections and adjournment.

Why this personality matters to the archive

Presiding officers often disappear in topic summaries because they are not always the loudest ideological speakers. In a chronological archive, chairing decisions, sequencing, recognition of members and procedural continuity are part of the constitutional story.

The portrait above is a verified later likeness, not a photograph of the December 1946 sitting. Debate claims on this page remain tied to indexed Assembly records.
