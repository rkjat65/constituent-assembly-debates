Permanent Chairman · Constituent Assembly

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

The visual archive first introduces Rajendra Prasad at the institutional moment when the Assembly elects its permanent presiding officer. Rather than treating him as a static biography card, this profile will grow with the sittings he chairs, the rulings he gives and the procedural transitions he oversees.

Indexed trajectory

11 DEC 1946

Elected permanent Chairman

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha declares Rajendra Prasad duly elected after the valid nomination process is read to the House.

11 DEC 1946

Conducted to the Chair

J. B. Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad conduct the elected President to the platform before the transition from the temporary Chair.

12 DEC 1946

Deliberation before debate

The archive indexes the sitting in which discussion on the Objectives Resolution is postponed so members can consider it before the next day's debate.

Why this personality matters to the archive

Presiding officers often disappear in topic summaries because they are not always the loudest ideological speakers. In a chronological archive, however, chairing decisions, sequencing, recognition of members and procedural continuity are part of the constitutional story.

This profile is intentionally limited to material already indexed in the archive. It will expand only as additional dated records are processed.
