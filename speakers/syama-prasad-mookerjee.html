Syama Prasad Mookerjee

The currently structured record first brings Mookerjee into sharp focus on 17 December 1946. He opposes postponing the Objectives Resolution and argues that the Assembly's ultimate political sanction comes from the people of India rather than from the absence or presence of any one political organisation.
