Jawaharlal Nehru

In the archive's opening sequence, Nehru appears first in procedural institution-building and then as the mover of the Objectives Resolution. His interventions provide an early bridge between how the Assembly works and what constitutional order it intends to build.

Interim rulesObjectives ResolutionPopular sovereigntyJustice & equalityRepublic

Indexed interventions

10 DEC 1946

Proposes interim procedural rules

Nehru moves that the Assembly provisionally use Central Legislative Assembly rules until it frames its own procedure.

13 DEC 1946

Moves the Objectives Resolution

He asks the Assembly to state clearly where it is going before it proceeds further into the detailed work of constitution-making.

DEC 1946

The Resolution becomes an Assembly-wide argument

The proposal is debated over multiple sittings, drawing questions about representation, safeguards, sovereignty and the character of the future political order.
