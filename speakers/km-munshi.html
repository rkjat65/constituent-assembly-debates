K. M. Munshi

In the currently processed record, Munshi becomes especially visible on 30 April 1947 as constitution-making moves from broad rights debate into organised structural work. 30 APR 1947

Turns political urgency into a work programme

Munshi presents the interim constitution-making programme at a moment when rapid political change makes a rigid timetable difficult. The emphasis is on parallel committee work and getting major constitutional principles back before the Assembly quickly.

30 APR 1947

Creates two major structural streams

He moves the resolutions authorising a Union Constitution Committee and a Provincial Constitution Committee, separating the next stage of structural design into dedicated bodies.

Why this matters institutionally

By late April, the Assembly is no longer only debating principles. It is designing a production system for the Constitution: specialised committees, parallel reports, plenary consideration and repeated revision. Munshi's indexed interventions are useful for seeing that machinery in operation.
