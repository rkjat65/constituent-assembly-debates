Sir Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar

Former Advocate General of Madras and one of the foremost constitutional jurists in the Constituent Assembly. A key member of the Drafting Committee and Sub-Committee on Fundamental Rights, Alladi played a pivotal role in formulating provisions on citizenship, judicial review, preventive detention, the emergency powers, and the distribution of legislative authority between the Union and the States.

How to read this personality

Sir Alladi was the master technician of constitutional jurisprudence. When ideological or political impasses arose, the Assembly frequently turned to Alladi to draft language that harmonised competing demands: balancing judicial supremacy with parliamentary authority, and State autonomy with national integrity.
