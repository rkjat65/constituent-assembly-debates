Temporary Chairman · Constituent Assembly

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

In the archive's opening sequence, Sinha is not merely a ceremonial figure. He presides over the Assembly's first sitting, delivers the inaugural address, manages early procedural questions and then oversees the transition to the permanent Chair.

Indexed interventions

09 DEC 1946

First sitting and inaugural address

Sinha takes the Chair after Kripalani's invitation, receives goodwill messages and places Indian constitution-making in comparative and historical perspective.

10 DEC 1946

Presiding over procedural design

As temporary Chairman, he conducts the sitting in which the Assembly works out election procedure, interim rules and the Rules Committee.

11 DEC 1946

Declares Rajendra Prasad elected

After reviewing the nomination papers, Sinha declares Rajendra Prasad duly elected permanent Chairman and presides over the transition.
