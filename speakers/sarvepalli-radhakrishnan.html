S. Radhakrishnan

In the currently processed record, Radhakrishnan becomes a major philosophical defender of the Objectives Resolution on 20 January 1947. He treats constitution-making as a peaceful transfer of political authority to the people and connects national unity with liberty, rights and a democratic constitutional order.

Constitution-making as peaceful transformation

Radhakrishnan defends the Objectives Resolution as a necessary declaration of the Assembly's direction and presents democratic constitution-making as an alternative to political transformation through force.
