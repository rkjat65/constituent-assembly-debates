# Personality Portrait Standard

The personality cards must use historically recognisable caricatures, not simplified cartoon avatars.

## Mandatory rules

- recognisable likeness before stylisation;
- facial proportions, age, hairstyle/headwear, spectacles, moustache and clothing must follow historical references;
- style: sepia pen-and-ink / engraved editorial caricature on warm parchment;
- caricature means selective emphasis of real features, not geometric distortion;
- no generic faces, vector-avatar geometry, anime/cartoon proportions, stock portraits or blurry photographic crops;
- tricolour and Ashoka motifs belong in the background, never on the face;
- every major personality gets one consistent master portrait reused across cards, session pages and profiles;
- if a trustworthy likeness is not ready, do not publish a placeholder portrait.

The approved visual reference is the realistic engraved/caricature treatment used in the Personality Explorer concept, where the person remains immediately recognisable.
