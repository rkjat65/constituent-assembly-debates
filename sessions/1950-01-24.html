The Final Sitting: Presidential Election, National Anthem & Signing Ceremony

24 January 195011:00 AMVolume XII · Constitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

President-elect of the Republic of India

Unanimously elected first President of India by the Constituent Assembly. Delivers the historic declaration on the National Anthem and National Song before leading the 284 members to sign the calligraphed Constitution.

View Prasad profile →
“
The composition consisting of the words and music known as Jana Gana Mana is the National Anthem of India, subject to such alterations in the words as the Government may authorise as occasion arises; and the song Vande Mataram, which has played a historic part in the struggle for Indian freedom, shall be honoured equally with Jana Gana Mana and shall have equal status with it.
RP
— Dr. Rajendra PrasadPresidential Declaration · 24 January 1950
☸

Historic milestones of the concluding sitting

  • 👑Election of the First President: Sri H. V. R. Iengar (Returning Officer and Assembly Secretary) announced that only one nomination had been received, proposing Dr. Rajendra Prasad, moved by Jawaharlal Nehru and seconded by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. Dr. Prasad was declared duly elected amidst thunderous applause.
  • 🎵Anthem and National Song Settlement: Dr. Prasad declared Rabindranath Tagore's Jana Gana Mana as the National Anthem of India, and Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay's Vande Mataram as possessing equal historic status as the National Song.
  • ✒Signing the Sacred Calligraphed Manuscript: Three historic master copies (English illuminated by Prem Behari Narain Raizada and Nandalal Bose, Hindi illuminated manuscript, and the printed English edition) were laid on the table. 284 members came forward to sign their names into eternity.
  • 🕊Solemn Finale: The Assembly concluded with the singing of Jana Gana Mana and Vande Mataram by Smt. Purnima Banerjee and other members, before adjourning sine die as the Constituent Assembly transformed into the Provisional Parliament of India on 26 January 1950.

✎ Prem Behari Narain Raizada

The entire text of the Constitution of India was handwritten in flowing italic calligraphy by Master Calligrapher Prem Behari Narain Raizada over six months without charging a single rupee, asking only to write his name and his grandfather's name on each page.

⚄ Nandalal Bose & Santiniketan

The borders and miniature paintings illustrating 5,000 years of Indian history (from Mohenjo-daro to Vedic times, Ashoka, Buddha, Akbar, Shivaji, Rani Lakshmibai, and Gandhi) were created by artists from Kala Bhavana, Santiniketan led by Nandalal Bose.

National Anthem Declaration

1 Mr. President (Dr. Rajendra Prasad): "There is one matter which has been pending... namely the question of the National Anthem. The composition consisting of the words and music known as Jana Gana Mana is the National Anthem of India... and the song Vande Mataram shall be honoured equally with Jana Gana Mana."

Election of First President of India

2 Returning Officer (H. V. R. Iengar): "Only one nomination paper has been received... proposing Dr. Rajendra Prasad... I hereby declare Dr. Rajendra Prasad to be duly elected to the office of President of India." Nehru and Patel escorted Dr. Prasad amidst tumultuous ovations.

Signing the Calligraphed Constitution

3 Mr. President: "Now we will proceed to sign the Constitution. Members will please come forward one by one in order." Jawaharlal Nehru was the first to sign, followed by Dr. Ambedkar, Sardar Patel, and the 284 members present.

The Birth of the Sovereign Democratic Republic

On 24 January 1950, three years, one month, and sixteen days of monumental constitutional deliberation culminated in the signing of India's supreme charter, coming into full force on 26 January 1950.
