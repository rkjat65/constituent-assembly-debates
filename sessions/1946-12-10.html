The Assembly Makes Its Rules

10 December 194611:00 AMConstitution Hall, New Delhi

J. B. Kripalani

Member, Constituent Assembly

United Provinces

“
We have assembled here, having no Rules of Procedure.
♙
— J. B. KripalaniCommittee on Rules of Procedure · 10 December 1946
⌂

What the Assembly had to decide

  • ✺How the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly would be elected.
  • ♧What rules would govern business until the Assembly framed its own procedure.
  • ◉How a Rules Committee would be constituted and what powers it would have.
  • ⌁Whether the Assembly’s rules should expressly cover its Sections and Committees.
♘

✎ Editor’s Note

This is constitution-making at the institutional level: before debating the final Constitution, the Assembly had to establish legitimate procedures for its own work.
