What becomes institutional today

  • ✺Rules of Procedure: the Assembly formally adopts the framework debated in Committee.
  • ◉Standing machinery: Credentials, House, and Finance & Staff Committees are constituted.
  • ⌁Federal Court: no member seeks a reference on the disputed May 16 interpretation, so the issue is not referred.
  • ♧Adjournment under the new Rules: the Assembly itself authorises the long adjournment to 20 January.

✎ An explicit source gap

During the Finance and Staff Committee issue, the transcript states that speeches were made and then expunged. The archive records the gap but does not invent the missing speakers or arguments.

⚄ Why this short sitting matters

The Assembly's first session ends with organisational capacity: rules, committees, election machinery and a procedurally valid route to reconvene. Institution-building is part of the constitutional history, not administrative filler.
