Two debates inside one sitting

  • ✺How should the Assembly work? Members contest Christmas scheduling and how much scrutiny the Rules deserve.
  • ◉Should the Resolution wait? Kunzru argues for delay; Gopalaswami Ayyangar urges completion now.
  • ⌁Who protects minorities? Nichols-Roy and Sidhwa answer British criticism from minority perspectives.
  • ♧Who is sovereign in the States? the debate reaches directly into princely-state constitutionalism.
