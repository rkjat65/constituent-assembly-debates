Appointment of the Drafting Committee

29 August 194710:00 AMVolume V · Constitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Chairman · Drafting Committee

Appointed to lead the seven-member Drafting Committee, beginning the monumental task of drafting the articles, schedules, and clauses of the Constitution of India.

View Ambedkar profile →
“
Resolved that a Committee consisting of the following members be appointed to scrutinise and to suggest necessary amendment to the Draft Constitution...
◫
— Resolution of the Assembly29 August 1947
⚖

The four pillars of the 29 August resolution

  • ◫The Seven Framers: Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar, N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, K. M. Munshi, Mohammad Saadulla, B. L. Mitter, and D. P. Khaitan.
  • 📜The Constitutional Adviser's Draft: Sir B. N. Rau's comprehensive 243-article draft and comparative constitutional studies serve as the working text for the committee.
  • ⚖Terms of Reference: The Committee is charged with scrutinising all committee reports, ensuring harmony between Union, Provincial, and Fundamental Rights provisions.
  • ✦Transition to Legal Precision: The Assembly concludes general ideological debate and charges the expert jurists with clause-by-clause legal formulation.

✎ The Engine Room of the Constitution

Over the next 141 days, the Drafting Committee held 141 sittings to review, re-write, and refine every single article, producing the historic Draft Constitution published in February 1948.

Resolution Moved

1 Shri Satyanarayan Sinha moves the formal resolution to appoint the Drafting Committee to scrutinise the draft of the Constitution prepared by the Constitutional Adviser.

Committee Composition

2 The seven members are proposed, combining distinguished legal scholars (Alladi, Ambedkar, Munshi), experienced administrators (Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Saadulla, Mitter), and industrial-legal experts (Khaitan).

Amendments & Scope

3 Members debate amendments concerning whether the committee should only rephrase approved principles or have the power to suggest new improvements. The Assembly confirms the Drafting Committee's wide mandate to recommend amendments necessary for constitutional integrity.

Unanimously Passed

4 Dr. Rajendra Prasad puts the resolution to vote. It is unanimously adopted, marking the formal commencement of the Drafting Committee's historic drafting mission.

From political principles to constitutional jurisprudence

The creation of the Drafting Committee marked the moment where political ideals and committee reports were entrusted to legal craftsmanship, setting the stage for India's enduring constitutional charter.
