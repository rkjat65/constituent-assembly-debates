Four threads close the January session

  • ✺Presiding continuity: H. C. Mookherjee becomes Vice-President, giving the Assembly an elected substitute for the President.
  • ◉Next-stage planning: a Business Committee is created to recommend the order of future constitutional work.
  • ⌁Federal architecture begins to narrow: a specialist committee is created to clarify the scope of subjects assigned to the Union Centre.
  • ♧Institutional privilege appears: the House records the seizure of Somnath Lahiri's Assembly papers and the President's request for legal advice.

✎ Inclusion vs. expertise

The Union-subject committee debate exposes a recurring constitutional design tension: should technical committees mirror the Assembly's social diversity, or primarily contain specialists? Jaipal Singh and Harnam Singh take sharply different positions.

⚄ A clean institutional boundary

When Lahiri reminds Rajendra Prasad that he also sits in the Interim Government, the President answers only in his capacity as presiding officer. That short exchange is unusually useful for studying institutional role separation.
