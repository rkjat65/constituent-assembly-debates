The Objectives Resolution Is Adopted

22 January 1947Volume IIConstitution Hall, New Delhi

Jawaharlal Nehru

Final reply · Objectives Resolution

Closes the debate after six weeks and argues that constitutional work can no longer be held up.

♙  Profile
“
There has been waiting enough.
JN
— Jawaharlal NehruFinal reply · Objectives Resolution
✓

What changes on this day

  • ✺Debate becomes commitment: the Resolution moves from six weeks of argument to a solemn standing vote.
  • ◉Popular sovereignty is defended: objections from princely rulers cannot displace the principle that authority ultimately comes from people.
  • ⌁Constitutional purpose becomes social: the final reply connects political freedom to hunger, poverty and opportunity.
  • ♧The Assembly keeps working: immediately after adoption it takes up Bhutan, Sikkim and its own budget machinery.

✎ Adoption is not the end of the story

The Objectives Resolution becomes a constitutional compass, not the finished Constitution. The archive will later connect its commitments to committee work, draft provisions and the final constitutional text.

⚄ Three languages before the vote

The Resolution is placed before the House in English, Hindi and Urdu immediately before members stand to vote. The multilingual sequence is part of the institutional record, not decorative translation.
