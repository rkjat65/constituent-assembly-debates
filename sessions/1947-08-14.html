The Midnight Session: Independence & Sovereign Power

14–15 August 194711:00 PM – MidnightVolume V · Constitution Hall, New Delhi

Jawaharlal Nehru

Leader of the House

Moves the historic motion that the Constituent Assembly assume power for the governance of India and delivers the 'Tryst with Destiny' address.

Long years ago we made a tryst with destiny, and now the time comes when we shall redeem our pledge, not wholly or in full measure, but very substantially.
JN
— Jawaharlal NehruMidnight Address · 14 August 1947
☸

The four acts of the Midnight Session

  • ☸Presidential Opening: Dr. Rajendra Prasad reflects on the solemn sacrifices of generations and reminds the Assembly of its immense moral responsibility to millions of ordinary citizens.
  • ✦Tryst with Destiny: Jawaharlal Nehru delivers the historic speech calling on the nation to pledge itself to the unending service of India and the ending of poverty, ignorance, and disease.
  • ✍The Midnight Pledge: Exactly at midnight, all members stand and take the solemn oath dedicating themselves to the service of the Indian Republic.
  • ⚑Presentation of the National Flag: Hansa Mehta presents the National Flag on behalf of the women of India as the emblem of free, sovereign democracy.

✎ The Assembly Becomes Sovereign

With the Indian Independence Act coming into effect at midnight, the Constituent Assembly ceased to be a British-constituted body and became the sovereign parliament and supreme constitution-making authority of India.

⚄ The Women's Offering

Hansa Mehta's presentation of the tricolour symbolised that India's freedom was forged by women and men alike, and that gender equality was integral to the new nation's character.

11:00 PM

1 The Assembly convenes under the presidency of Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Vande Mataram is sung. Prasad opens the session acknowledging the triumph of non-violent struggle and paying homage to martyrs and the Father of the Nation, Mahatma Gandhi.

Nehru's Address

2 Jawaharlal Nehru moves the historic resolution and delivers his address: "A moment comes, which comes but rarely in history, when we step out from the old to the new, when an age ends, and when the soul of a nation, long suppressed, finds utterance."

Radhakrishnan

3 Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan seconds the resolution with a philosophical treatise on power, warning that political freedom without economic emancipation and moral purification can easily decay into self-interest.

Midnight Pledge

4 As midnight strikes, the President and all Members stand and repeat the solemn Pledge: "I, being a member of the Constituent Assembly of India, do dedicate myself in all humility to the service of India and her people..."

National Flag

5 Smt. Hansa Mehta rises and presents the National Flag to the Assembly: "It is in the fitness of things that this first flag that is to fly over this august House should be a gift from the women of India." The House receives the flag amidst prolonged cheers.

From subjecthood to democratic sovereignty

The midnight session of 14–15 August 1947 fundamentally transformed the Constituent Assembly from an imperial consultative council into the supreme democratic sovereign authority of free India.
