A Permanent President Is Elected

11 December 194611:00 AMConstitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

Temporary Chairman

♙  View Speaker Profile
“
I hereby declare the Hon'ble Dr. Rajendra Prasad, as the duly elected permanent Chairman.
♙
— Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha11 December 1946

Why this sitting matters

  • ✺The Assembly moves from a temporary Chair to an elected permanent presiding officer.
  • ♧J. B. Kripalani is proposer and Vallabhbhai Patel seconder in a valid nomination.
  • ◉With no competing valid nomination, Rajendra Prasad is declared duly elected.
  • ⌁Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad conduct him to the Chair.

⚄ The transition

Sinha invites Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad to bring the elected President to the platform. After congratulations, Rajendra Prasad assumes the Chair.

Debate Transcript (Visual Structure)

View Full Debate →
Election

The valid nomination papers for Rajendra Prasad are read and the absence of another valid nomination is recorded.

Declared

Sinha declares Rajendra Prasad duly elected permanent Chairman.

Escort

Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad conduct Rajendra Prasad to the Chair.

Chair

After congratulatory speeches, Rajendra Prasad assumes the Chair.
