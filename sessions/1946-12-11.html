A Permanent President Is Elected

11 December 194611:00 AMConstitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

Temporary Chairman

Presiding at the opening of the sitting

♙  View Speaker Profile
“
I hereby declare the Hon'ble Dr. Rajendra Prasad, as the duly elected permanent Chairman.
♙
— Dr. Sachchidananda SinhaElection of the Permanent Chairman · 11 December 1946
♜

Why this sitting matters

  • ✺The Assembly moves from a temporary Chair to an elected permanent presiding officer.
  • ♧One valid nomination lists J. B. Kripalani as proposer and Vallabhbhai Patel as seconder.
  • ◉With no competing valid nomination, Rajendra Prasad is declared duly elected.
  • ⌁Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad conduct him to the Chair before congratulations begin.
♘

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

⚄ The transition

Sinha first invites Kripalani and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad to bring the elected President to the platform. After congratulations, Sinha vacates the Chair and Rajendra Prasad assumes it.
