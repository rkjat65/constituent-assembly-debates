The Munshi-Ayyangar Formula: Part XVII Official Language Settlement

14 September 194910:00 AMVolume IX · Constitution Hall, New Delhi

K. M. Munshi & N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar

Joint Architects of the Language Compromise

Draft the compromise formula that preserved national cohesion: Hindi in Devanagari script for official purposes, international form of Indian numerals, and continued English use for 15 years.

View Munshi profile →
“
The official language of the Union shall be Hindi in Devanagari script. The form of numerals to be used for the official purposes of the Union shall be the international form of Indian numerals... For a period of fifteen years from the commencement of this Constitution, the English language shall continue to be used for all official purposes.
GA
— N. Gopalaswami AyyangarLanguage Settlement Resolution · 14 September 1949
☸

Pillars of the Language Consensus

  • 📜The Munshi-Ayyangar Compromise: Navigated deep divides between northern delegates demanding immediate total Hindi and southern/eastern members advocating regional language parity and gradual transition.
  • 🔢International Form of Indian Numerals: Ensured scientific, technological, and global commercial interoperability while recognizing the Indian historical origin of the decimal numerals (1, 2, 3...).
  • 🗣Eighth Schedule Safeguard: Recognized all major regional languages as national languages of India, guaranteeing state legislatures the right to adopt official languages for provincial administration.
  • 🏛Article 351 Directive: Obligated the Union to promote Hindi enriched by Hindustani and all other Indian languages specified in the Eighth Schedule.

✎ Hindi Diwas Origin

14 September is celebrated nationwide across India as Hindi Diwas to commemorate the adoption of the Munshi-Ayyangar formula by the Constituent Assembly on 14 September 1949.

⚄ Composite Hindustani

Maulana Azad passionately urged that the national language must embody the composite cultural synthesis (Ganga-Jamuni tehzeeb) of all India's regions.

Ayyangar Moves Consensus Formula

1 N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar: "We have landed on a solution which has very nearly the unanimous support of the Assembly... The decision arrived at was that Hindi should be the official language of the Union, with international numerals, and that English should continue for 15 years."

Nehru's Plea for Generosity

2 Jawaharlal Nehru: "If you want Hindi to be the all-India language, it can only be so by consensus, by winning the hearts of non-Hindi speaking peoples, not by imposing it or showing an authoritarian tone. Great languages grow by inclusion and receptivity."

Dr. Rajendra Prasad Puts Motion

3 Dr. Rajendra Prasad: "I congratulate the House on having arrived at an agreed solution on this most difficult and contentious problem. Let us all work together to make our national languages flourish." The formula was adopted by acclamation.
