Three constitutional problems in one sitting

  • ✺Who represents the princely States? Members contest rulers, peoples, direct election and sectional claims.
  • ◉Who has final authority? The negotiating committee is made answerable to the Assembly.
  • ⌁What happens to the Objectives Resolution? Further debate is suspended so organisational work can proceed.
  • ♧How does a sovereign Assembly govern itself? Munshi presents rules on dissolution, language, committees, rights and procedure.
