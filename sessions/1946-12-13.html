The Objectives Resolution

13 December 1946Constitution Hall, New DelhiVolume I

Jawaharlal Nehru

Member, Constituent Assembly

United Provinces

“
This Constituent Assembly declares its firm and solemn resolve to proclaim India as an Independent Sovereign Republic...
♙
— Jawaharlal Nehru13 December 1946
⌂

Summary of Key Points

  • ✺Nehru introduces the Objectives Resolution as a statement of the Assembly’s constitutional aims.
  • ♧The Resolution locates authority in the people and imagines an independent sovereign polity.
  • ◉It articulates justice, equality and safeguards for minorities and backward areas.
  • ⌁Its ideas later informed the Preamble and the Constitution’s political philosophy.
