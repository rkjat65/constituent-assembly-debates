Minorities, the Common Citizen and the End of Postponement

21 January 1947Volume IIConstitution Hall, New Delhi

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

President · Constituent Assembly

Runs the election, controls the amendment dispute and schedules completion of the Resolution.

♙  Profile
“
We are a sovereign body, but let us approach our task ... in the spirit of negotiators.
SP
— S. H. PraterOn minority protection and constitutional agreement
◎

Four constitutional tensions in one sitting

  • ✺Unity vs. delay: speakers argue the Assembly has waited long enough for absent participants.
  • ◉Liberty vs. state power: Jerome D'Souza warns that patriotic reform can become regimentation.
  • ⌁Minority safeguards vs. homogeneity: cultural and political difference is treated as something to protect, not merely absorb.
  • ♧Principles vs. institutions: speakers ask how justice, representation and popular sovereignty will actually be enforced.

✎ The common citizen enters the frame

Pataskar warns that a scramble for separate sectional charters could make constitution-makers lose sight of a common constitutional guarantee for ordinary people.

⚄ Postponement reaches its procedural end

Jayakar says the Assembly already granted the delay he sought. The Chair then prevents withdrawal from becoming a route to introduce fresh proposals and the amendment leaves the floor.
