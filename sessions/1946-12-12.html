Why this short sitting matters

  • ✺The Chair recognises that members need more time to consider a major constitutional Resolution.
  • ◉The House agrees to postpone the discussion to 11:00 AM on 13 December.
  • ⌁Members are invited to submit amendments so they can be circulated before debate.
