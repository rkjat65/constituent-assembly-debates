10 DEC 1946Procedure

Rules Committee

The early institutional body responsible for preparing the rules by which the Assembly would govern its own proceedings.

Why it matters

Before constitutional substance can be debated, the Assembly needs rules for motions, amendments, elections, committees and adjournment.

21 DEC 1946 → 28 APR 1947States

States Negotiating Committee

Created to negotiate representation of Indian States. By April its work has produced an agreed route for State representatives to begin entering the Assembly.

Thread

Its January scope expands to Bhutan and Sikkim. On 28 April Nehru presents the negotiations to the House: seat distribution, representation procedure and the compromise between elected and government-linked State representation.

Finance & Staff Committee

Prepares the Assembly's expenditure and member-allowance proposals that return to the House in January.

20 JAN → 30 APR 1947Business control

Steering Committee

Coordinates the Assembly's working programme and gradually incorporates representatives of Indian States into the Assembly's central business machinery.

Thread

Two State-reserved places are opened on 28 April. On 30 April P. Govinda Menon and C. S. Venkatachar are declared elected after being the only two nominees.

Resolution →Election →States seats opened →State members elected →
24 JAN → 30 APR 1947Rights & safeguards

Advisory Committee

The major body charged with developing recommendations on fundamental rights, minority safeguards and tribal/excluded areas.

Report stage

After its January formation, the committee's interim Fundamental Rights work reaches the House on 29 April and moves clause by clause on 30 April through equality, titles and freedoms.

Formation debate →Interim rights report →Clause-by-clause work →Rights theme →
25 JAN → 30 APR 1947Next-stage planning

Business Committee

A compact three-member body created to recommend the order of further constitution-making business before the Assembly resumed.

Report stage

Its interim programme returns on 30 April. Rapid political change prevents a final programme, but the report proposes parallel committee work and a compressed schedule for bringing major constitutional principles back to the House.

Formation debate →Interim programme →
25 JAN → 28 APR 1947Federal design

Committee on Subjects Assigned to the Union Centre

Created to work through the scope and interconnection of subjects assigned to the Union. Its first report is presented on 28 April but deliberately not treated as final.

Report stage

Gopalaswami Ayyangar says League participation, fuller States representation and the unsettled political future may require the Centre's subjects, concurrent field and Union–unit relations to be re-examined in collaboration with later constitutional committees.

Formation debate →Report presented →
30 APR 1947Union structure

Union Constitution Committee

Authorised as a committee of not more than fifteen members to report on the main principles of the Union Constitution before the Assembly's next session.

Why it matters

The Assembly now separates the high-level structure of the Union from the narrower Union-subject exercise, while still expecting the different committee streams to inform one another.

Creation resolution →Union formation theme →
30 APR 1947Provincial structure

Provincial Constitution Committee

Authorised as a committee of not more than twenty-five members to report on the main principles of a model Provincial Constitution.

Questions immediately visible

The creation debate already points toward Chief Commissioners' provinces, Coorg and linguistic reorganisation, showing that a "model" provincial constitution will have to confront territorial diversity.

The institutional build so far

10 DEC
Rules first

The Assembly creates procedure before expanding into specialised constitutional work.

21–23 DEC
States + internal administration

Negotiation with States and the Assembly's own credentials, housing, finance and staff machinery come into view.

20–25 JAN
Rights, steering and federal preparation

The Advisory, Steering, Business and Union-subject structures turn broad constitutional aims into specialised work programmes.

28–30 APR
Reports return and structural drafting branches out

States enter the Assembly, rights clauses reach the House, State representatives join the Steering Committee and two new bodies are authorised to define Union and provincial constitutional principles in parallel.
